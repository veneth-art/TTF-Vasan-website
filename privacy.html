Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy

An honest disclosure of data handling for this static fan website.

In Plain English

This website is an independent, static, non-commercial fan tribute. We do not require accounts, we do not run user databases, we do not deploy advertising tracking scripts, and we do not sell or monetize personal user data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Because this is a client-side static website:

  • We do not require you to create an account or provide personal credentials.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • We do not process credit cards, payments, or financial transactions.
  • We do not run tracking analytics suites or commercial surveillance scripts.

2. Third-Party Services & Content Delivery

To deliver content efficiently and host multimedia, this site interacts with trusted third-party providers:

  • YouTube (Google LLC): We feature video embeds and thumbnails hosted on YouTube from the channel @TwinThrottlers_TTF. When you load a page containing a video embed or play a video, YouTube may collect device and usage data according to Google's Privacy Policy. We configure lazy-loading for iframes so embed connections only initialize as needed.
  • Google Fonts: Typography (Space Grotesk and Inter) is served via Google's font CDN. Your browser downloads font files directly from Google's servers, which may log basic HTTP request headers (such as IP address and user agent) for resource delivery.
  • Lucide Icons (unpkg CDN): Icon graphics are delivered via the unpkg CDN for interface rendering.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

This website does not set any first-party cookies for advertising, tracking, or profiling.

Your browser may locally cache standard static assets (HTML, CSS, JavaScript files, and images) to speed up subsequent page visits. You can clear your browser cache at any time through your browser's settings.

4. External Links

Our website includes hyperlinks to external third-party sites, including YouTube, Instagram, and news publishers. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of those third-party websites. We encourage you to review their respective privacy notices upon leaving our site.

5. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for general audiences interested in motorcycling and content creation. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

6. Updates to This Policy

If any structural modifications or new static features are introduced to this fan website, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.

7. Contact & Inquiries

For any privacy inquiries or suggestions regarding this policy, please open an issue in the project's source code repository or reach out via our community discussion spaces.
