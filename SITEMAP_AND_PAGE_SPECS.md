# Sitemap and Page Specs

## Global
Persistent “Unofficial fan-made website” label; responsive navigation; footer with disclaimer and privacy link. No unverified photos or bikes.

## Home
Text-led hero “THE RIDE. THE JOURNEY. THE STORY.”; optional authentic TTF Vasan image only if identity, rights, and source pass review; featured story; permitted video links/embeds; sourced timeline preview; latest news.

## Videos
Permitted embeds or direct source links only. Show title, source, verified date when available. Do not rehost copyrighted clips without permission.

## Biography
Sourced timeline. Link factual claims to reliable sources. Do not invent dates or details.

## News
Original summaries, date, source link, editorial review, and deduplication. Separate confirmed reporting from opinion or unverified submissions.

## About
Explain fan-made status and no official affiliation or endorsement.

## Privacy
Describe only data actually collected by the implementation.

## Image fallback
When authentic, verifiable, appropriately usable imagery is unavailable, use typography or abstract visuals. Never substitute unrelated photography.
