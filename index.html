Key Career Milestones

Chronological milestones formatted year-to-year — verified against primary channel uploads and public news records.

2019 – 2020

Birth of Twin Throttlers & Jarvis Build

Launched the channel with custom modifications on his Yamaha R15 V3 'Jarvis', bringing energetic Tamil motovlog commentary to the forefront.

Source: Twin Throttlers
2021 – 2022

3,500 km Ladakh Road Trip Expedition

The marathon multi-state motorcycle road trip from Coimbatore to Leh Ladakh catapulted him to nationwide digital stardom.

Source: Ladakh Vlog Series
2022 – 2023

Superbike Fleet: Z900 & Hayabusa

Acquired the Kawasaki Z900 and fulfilled his childhood dream of taking delivery of the Suzuki Hayabusa GSX-1300R superbike.

Source: Superbike Delivery Vlogs
2023 – 2024

RACR Circuit Training & Cinema Announcements

Underwent professional track training at RACR to champion closed-circuit safety, and announced initial feature film acting ventures in Tamil cinema.

Source: RACR & Film Dispatches
2024 – 2025

IPL (Indian Penal Law) – Film First Look & Production

TTF Vasan's Tamil cinema transition took shape with IPL (Indian Penal Law). The first look was unveiled on 18 November 2024, ahead of the film's theatrical release on 28 November 2025.

Source: DT Next & Wikipedia
2025 – 2026

Marriage Celebration & Family Milestone

Married Prakashini and celebrated his wedding reception and first anniversary with the TTF Family, sharing personal milestones alongside lifestyle and touring content.

Source: Reception Video

5.13M+ Channel Milestone
